The 38 garnets, as structures to be determined

Zakaria Kharbouch's thesis made 38 Ga-stabilised LLZO garnets in two families and refined two quantities per garnet, the lattice parameter and the phase fractions, on laboratory Cu Kα data. Every argument it builds on them is about lithium and about which site each dopant takes. This page shows what each probe can see in these garnets, what every one of the 38 compositions will be measured with, and which structural question each measurement answers.

Who can be told apart, site by site

A powder pattern measures a site's total scattering. X-rays weigh each atom by its electrons, Z; neutrons by its coherent scattering length, b, which does not follow Z and is negative for lithium and hydrogen. Two occupants of a site can be separated only by a probe in which they differ.
