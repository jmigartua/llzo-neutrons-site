What is known, and what would be new

The average structure of Ga-LLZO has been measured with neutrons: Ga on 12a of \(I\bar{4}3d\) (Wagner et al. 2016, SPODI; Rettenwander et al. 2016, ILL D20), Ga–Ta LLZO made with the thesis's own 15 % lithium excess (Christopher et al. 2023, ECHIDNA), Ta–Ba–Ga LLZO (Meesala et al. 2019). A proposal that offered "the first neutron study of Ga-LLZO" would not survive the panel. What no one has done, and the thesis's samples allow:

  • Scandium on the zirconium site refined from neutron data: the Ga–Sc garnets have been made and measured (Buannic et al. 2017), never with neutrons, although Sc has the strongest contrast of the cell.
  • A direct assignment of the yttrium site in a garnet electrolyte, by EXAFS and resonant diffraction: so far argued from lattice parameters only (Hitz et al. 2013, and the thesis).
  • Rubidium on the lanthanum site up to 0.30, six times the published level (Wu et al. 2017), and whether it survives sintering.
  • A systematic matrix at fixed processing: three La-site substituents of charge 1, 2 and 3 against two Zr-site co-dopants of charge 5 and 3, on one Ga backbone, so that the effect of each on the lithium sublattice and on the \(Ia\bar{3}d\)/\(I\bar{4}3d\) choice is separated.
  • The contested space group at 0.2 Ga: \(I\bar{4}3d\) by neutrons and single crystals, \(Ia\bar{3}d\) by synchrotron and NMR at the thesis's exact composition (Vema et al. 2023), with protonation as another cause of acentricity (Orera et al. 2016). Fresh, dry samples with joint X-ray and neutron data can settle it.
  • The balance of every element: how much of the nominal Ga is in the garnet rather than in \(\mathrm{LiGaO_{2}}\), how much Al came from the boats, where the Zr of the \(\mathrm{Li_{2}ZrO_{3}}\) came from.

The application is the one the thesis aims at: solid electrolytes for lithium-metal all-solid-state batteries. The structural results become design rules (how much lithium each substituent really adds, where it goes, what stabilises the cubic phase) and the structure-aware descriptors that the thesis's machine-learning chapter asks for.

The sample sets

K12

The key set: twelve compositions that carry the thesis's questions

  • Ga–Ta reference (x = 0) and Ga–Sc reference: the two baselines.
  • Ga–Y–Ta at x = 0.10, 0.20, 0.30: the best conductor of the thesis (x = 0.20) between its neighbours; the Y-on-Zr question.
  • Ga–Ba–Ta at x = 0.10 and 0.20: below and above the Ba solubility limit.
  • Ga–Rb–Ta at x = 0.05 and 0.20: high and low tetragonal fraction, the Li-rich end.
  • Ga–Rb–Sc at x = 0.03 and 0.20: the best conductor of the Sc family and its decline.
  • Ga–Y–Sc at x = 0.10: Y in the other family, read by the thesis as on the La site.
Why these

Each pair or triple isolates one structural question (G01–G09) at fixed processing; together they cover both families, the three substituents and both best compositions.

A38

The whole matrix: all 38 compositions

  • 19 Ga–M–Ta and 19 Ga–M–Sc compositions, x = 0, 0.03, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30 for M = Ba, Rb, Y.
Why these

One protocol, one instrument for the trends of the thesis; the ML chapter's training set measured with structure.

The proposals

P0UM6P (LIMSET) and EHUanswers G02 G03 G08 G09

At home, before beam time: samples that can answer

Instrument

ICP-OES; TGA–MS; laboratory XRD with an internal standard; glove box; EIS to higher frequency or lower temperature

Route

No proposal: the groups' own means, from November 2026

Samples

K12 re-synthesised fresh by the thesis's M-SSR route, as powders (\(\ge 3\) g each) and sintered pellets; the stored powders of the thesis kept for comparison.

Request

—

What is measured and how
  • Li, Ga, Al, La, Zr and M by ICP-OES on powders and pellets: the constraint every Li occupancy is refined under.
  • TGA–MS (\(\mathrm{H_{2}O}\), \(\mathrm{CO_{2}}\)) on the same powders: protonation and carbonate.
  • Laboratory XRD with Si (NIST 640) as internal standard: lattice parameters with esd and zero shift stated, before and after storage.
  • Storage under argon from synthesis to beam; a deliberately aged twin of Ga–Ta and Ga–Y₀.₂₀–Ta.
  • The decision on ⁷Li: natural Li for the matrix; ⁷\(\mathrm{Li_{2}CO_{3}}\) for two or three compositions if the D2B data ask for it.
Expected results

Samples whose composition is known and whose history is recorded; the preliminary data every proposal needs.

Why this instrument

Panels fund questions, not samples. The ICP and TGA numbers turn "we want to see the Li" into "the nominal Li is 6.5, ICP gives x, neutrons will say where it is".

Risk and its answer

Re-synthesis may not reproduce the thesis's phase fractions exactly; that is itself a result (reproducibility), and the stored powders measured beside the fresh ones say how much.

Deadline

Before mid-February 2027

P1ILL, Grenobleanswers G01 G02 G03 G05 G06 G08

Where the lithium sits: high-resolution neutron diffraction of the key set

Instrument

D2B (λ = 1.594 Å, Δd/d \(\approx\) \(5\times10^{-4}\), 2\(\theta\) to 160°); alternative D20 in its high-resolution Ge configuration

Route

ILL standard proposal, College 5 (materials), with UPV/EHU proposers ensuring the two-thirds member-country rule; cross-referenced to P2

Samples

K12 (fresh, from P0), 2–3 g each, natural Li, in 8 mm vanadium cans; two sintered pellets crushed (Ga–Y₀.₂₀–Ta, Ga–Rb₀.₀₃–Sc).

Request

\(\approx 8\) shifts (2.7 days): about 4 h per sample for 14 samples, plus empty can, vanadium and Si standard. Estimate.

What is measured and how
  • Full patterns to \(d \approx 0.81\) Å at room temperature.
  • Joint Rietveld refinement with the ID22 pattern of the same powder (P2) in diff-ref-struct: lattice and profile from X-rays, Li/Ga/Al occupancies, O1/O2 coordinates and displacement parameters from neutrons.
  • Constraints: ICP composition (P0), charge balance, site capacities.
Expected results

For each composition: Li over 12a, 12b and 48e with esd (the measured vacancy concentration); Ga on 12a; Al or its upper bound; the acentric O distortion; Ba on the La site for the Ba series; H from the background. The vacancy argument of the thesis tested against measured Li contents.

Why this instrument

Lithium and oxygen carry 57 % of the neutron scattering power of the garnet against 6 % in X-rays; every Li question is 2–4 times more visible (Contrast page). D2B's resolution separates the cubic and tetragonal garnets and \(\mathrm{Li_{2}ZrO_{3}}\).

Risk and its answer

Absorption of natural Li (\(\mu \approx 1.5\) \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\), \(\mu R \approx 0.37\) in an 8 mm can): correctable, but ⁷Li (μ \(\approx 0.12\) \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\)) would remove it for the two compositions where the Li occupancy matters most.

Deadline

15 February 2027 (ILL standard round)

P2ESRF, Grenobleanswers G04 G05 G06 G07 G09

All 38, powders and pellets: high-resolution synchrotron diffraction

Instrument

ID22 (31–35 keV, 13-channel analyser, FWHM \(\approx 0.0025\)°, robot for 75 capillaries); alternative BM25 SpLine through the Spanish CRG route

Route

ESRF standard proposal, UPV/EHU as main proposer; or a BAG with UPV/EHU as PI and at least three groups

Samples

A38 calcined powders and K12 crushed pellets, 0.5 mm capillaries, with an internal standard for the amorphous fraction; \(\mathrm{LaB_{6}}\) / Si for calibration.

Request

\(\approx 6\) shifts: 50 patterns of a few tens of minutes with the robot, plus resonant data at the Y K edge (17.04 keV) for 3 compositions at 3 energies. Estimate.

What is measured and how
  • Lattice parameters with esd for all 38 under one protocol (the two chapters on one footing).
  • Phase fractions (c-LLZO, t-LLZO, \(\mathrm{Li_{2}ZrO_{3}}\), \(\mathrm{BaZrO_{3}}\), \(\mathrm{Sc_{2}O_{3}}\), \(\mathrm{Y_{2}O_{3}}\), \(\mathrm{La_{2}Zr_{2}O_{7}}\)) with detection limits \(\approx 0.1\) wt%, for powders and pellets.
  • La-site occupancy (Y, Rb on La) under the ICP constraint; weak \(I\bar{4}3d\) reflections; peak shapes (strain, compositional spread).
  • Resonant diffraction below and at the Y K edge: the Y contrast changed on purpose, site by site.
Expected results

The structural table of the thesis remade with esd; a closed mass balance for each composition; the powder-to-pellet change of the phase state; the fraction of Y missing from the La site.

Why this instrument

Resolution and statistics no laboratory instrument reaches; the X-ray contrast is the right one for Y, Rb, Ta and Sc on their sites (Contrast page).

Risk and its answer

Rb, Ba and La absorb at 31–35 keV in 0.5 mm capillaries; diluted or thinner capillaries, or 60 keV, if needed.

Deadline

1 March 2027 (ESRF standard round)

P3ILL, Grenobleanswers G02 G05 G09 G10

Temperature and the whole matrix: neutron thermodiffraction

Instrument

D20 (1.30 Å, \(\approx\) \(10^{8}\) n \(\mathrm{cm}^{-2}\) \(\mathrm{s}^{-1}\); furnace to 1100 °C); alternatives through the Spanish CRG routes: D1B (CNRS–CSIC) and XtremeD (INMA–CSIC), both with furnaces to 1200 K

Route

ILL standard proposal; or the D1B / XtremeD CRG calls (Spanish share)

Samples

A38 at room temperature (the compositions not in P1 for Li trends across the matrix); four compositions heated to 1000 K: Ga–Ta, Ga–Y₀.₂₀–Ta, Ga–Rb₀.₀₃–Sc, Ga–Rb₀.₂₀–Ta (tetragonal–cubic transition).

Request

\(\approx 6\) shifts: about 15 min per room-temperature pattern, 6–8 h per heating ramp. Estimate.

What is measured and how
  • Room temperature: Li content and tetragonal fraction across the 38, at lower resolution than D2B but with the same contrast.
  • Ramps to 1000 K: lattice expansion, the tetragonal–cubic transition, Li loss in situ, high-temperature datasets for nuclear density maps of the Li pathways.
Expected results

The Li-content trend of every series; the tetragonal–cubic transition temperature against the measured Li content; Li density maps at 300, 600 and 900 K for the four compositions.

Why this instrument

D20's flux makes the whole matrix and the ramps possible in days. The Spanish CRG instruments give a second route if the ILL round is tight.

Risk and its answer

Li volatilisation above 900 °C in the furnace: closed Nb or Pt containers, and the ramp stopped below the sintering temperature.

Deadline

15 February 2027 (ILL); spring CRG calls (D1B, XtremeD)

P4ESRF, Grenobleanswers G04 G06 G07

Where Y, Rb, Ga and Sc sit locally: X-ray absorption spectroscopy

Instrument

BM23 (4.5–90 keV, multi-sample holders); BM08 LISA or BM20 for the Sc K edge (4.49 keV); BM25 SpLine (Spanish route) for edges above 5 keV

Route

ESRF standard proposal; BM25 through the Spanish CRG committee (March and September calls)

Samples

Ga–Y–Ta (x = 0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30), Ga–Y–Sc (0.05, 0.20), Ga–Rb–Ta (0.20), Ga–Rb–Sc (0.03, 0.20), the two references; references \(\mathrm{Y_{2}O_{3}}\), YSZ, \(\mathrm{Y_{3}Al_{5}O_{12}}\), \(\mathrm{RbNO_{3}}\) or \(\mathrm{Rb_{2}CO_{3}}\), \(\mathrm{Ga_{2}O_{3}}\), \(\mathrm{LiGaO_{2}}\), \(\mathrm{Sc_{2}O_{3}}\). Pellets diluted in cellulose or BN, transmission.

Request

\(\approx 9\) shifts: Y K (17.04 keV) about 10 samples, Rb K (15.20 keV) 4, Ga K (10.37 keV) 6, Zr K (18.0 keV) 4, with references. Sc K separately on BM08 or BM20. Estimate.

What is measured and how
  • Y K EXAFS: Y–O coordination number and distance, Y–cation shells: 6-coordinated Zr site against 8-coordinated La site.
  • Rb K: the Rb environment in the garnet against Rb-rich second phases.
  • Ga K: tetrahedral Ga, Ga–O distance, the local picture behind the 12a occupancy of P1.
  • Zr K: the octahedral site as seen from Zr, for the Y and Sc series.
Expected results

The Y site fractions of G04 measured locally; Rb and Ga environments confirmed; the Sc site.

Why this instrument

Only a local, element-specific probe can tell Y on the Zr site from Y on the La site; neither diffraction probe can (Contrast page, cases y-zr and y-la).

Risk and its answer

Ba L3 is cut by the La L3 edge 236 eV above it and Ta L3 by Ga K: Ba and Ta only by K-edge (37.4 and 67.4 keV) if needed, or not at all in the first round.

Deadline

1 March 2027 (ESRF); March call (BM25)

P5ESRF and ILLanswers G09 G11

Second round: local order, and the synthesis watched in situ

Instrument

ID15A (X-ray PDF; oven to 1200 K), ILL D4c (neutron PDF); BM31 (XAS + XRD + PDF in situ) or ID15A for the synthesis

Route

After P1–P4 results; September 2027 rounds

Samples

Ga–Ta, Ga–Y₀.₂₀–Ta, Ga–Rb₀.₀₃–Sc for PDF; the precursor mixture of Ga–Y₀.₂₀–Ta for the synthesis in situ (\(450 \to 900\) °C).

Request

\(\approx 3\) shifts ID15A, \(\approx 2\) days D4c. Estimate.

What is measured and how
  • X-ray and neutron PDF: local distortions around Y, Rb, Ga; Li–O and Li–Li correlations.
  • In situ synthesis: the order in which \(\mathrm{La_{2}Zr_{2}O_{7}}\), \(\mathrm{Li_{2}ZrO_{3}}\), t- and c-LLZO form along the thesis's M-SSR route, and where lithium is lost.
Expected results

The local structure behind the average one; the step of the synthesis where the \(\mathrm{Li_{2}ZrO_{3}}\) of G09 forms.

Why this instrument

It answers what P1–P4 leave open, and connects the thesis's synthesis chapter (Chapter II) to the structures.

Risk and its answer

Only worth asking once P1–P2 have shown which compositions carry the effects.

Deadline

September 2027 rounds

The companion project: the cathodes

Fadila El Kouihen's thesis on doped Ni-rich layered cathodes (UM6P, same supervisors) is worked the same way in its own project, ni-rich-neutrons: there the neutron question is which metal sits in the lithium layer, nickel or manganese. The two sets of proposals cite each other, and together they cover the electrolyte and the cathode of an all-solid-state battery.
