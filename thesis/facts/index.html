How to read it

  • Nominal Li assumes M on the La site; Y on the Zr site would add one Li per Y. The vacancies are \(7 - \mathrm{Li}\); the literature's critical value for the cubic garnet is 0.4–0.5 (thesis, p. 63).
  • Past full lithium. Ga–Rb–Sc at x = 0.20 and 0.30 are weighed with 6.9 and 7.1 Li per formula unit: no vacancies left at all, and at x = 0.30 more lithium than fully stuffed LLZO holds. Either part of the Rb or the Li does not enter the garnet, or it is tetragonal; the thesis sees only traces of tetragonal garnet. The measured Li content (G02) and the Rb on the La site (G06) will say which.
  • Two families, two footings. The Ga–M–Ta lattice parameters come from the V4.0 refinements with all phases; the Ga–M–Sc ones from the earlier protocol, two decimals, 0.06–0.09 Å higher. Compare within a family, not across.
  • Powders and pellets. The structural columns are for calcined powders, the density and conductivity columns for sintered pellets.
  • Where the beam time will add columns: refined Li per formula unit and its distribution (12a, 12b, 48e), Ga and Al on 12a, Ba or Rb or Y on the La site, Y on the Zr site, the acentric distortion, phase fractions of pellets, ICP composition, H content, all with esd.
